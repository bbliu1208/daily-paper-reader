## 摘要
凌星褐矮星为亚恒星演化模型提供了直接的经验检验，目前已知超过50颗，使我们能够在群体层面进行这一检验。我们将31颗场龄、弱辐照凌星褐矮星的观测质量与半径与Sonora Diamondback、Sonora Bobcat和ATMO 2020网格进行了比较，并将其中的23颗与Mukherjee等人（2026）的辐照模型进行了比较。在每次比较中，我们都发现了一个依赖于半径的趋势，R_pred与R_obs的拟合斜率为0.3-0.4，比一一对应关系更浅，显著性为3.7-8σ。平均膨胀率为5-14%，取决于网格，在最大的天体中达到约20%。我们还测量到8-12%的内禀弥散，是测量不确定度的两到三倍。虽然平均膨胀偏移在不同网格间相差三倍，但这一弥散并未随之变化，表明它是群体属性而非模型属性。我们发现其与质量、平衡温度、轨道间距、潮汐加热速率或年龄均无相关性，仅与主星金属丰度存在微弱的（1.7σ）趋势。最值得注意的是与辐照没有任何趋势，这反对将类热木星的恒星加热作为主要原因。我们反而认为，褐矮星的大气边界条件范围比当前网格所代表的更广。褐矮星的半径膨胀与全对流M矮星相当，并且很可能是动力学质量基准中光度差异的凌星对应体。如果这一趋势延伸到氘燃烧极限以下，将会使直接成像行星的质量估计产生偏差，将光度-年龄质量高估几个到十个百分点，并要求将光谱-重力质量上调约20-45%。

## Abstract
Transiting brown dwarfs provide a direct empirical test of substellar evolutionary models, and with more than 50 now known we can carry out this test at the population level. We compared the observed masses and radii of 31 field-age, weakly irradiated transiting brown dwarfs to the Sonora Diamondback, Sonora Bobcat, and ATMO 2020 grids, and a subset of 23 of them to the irradiated models of Mukherjee et al. (2026). In every comparison we find a radius-dependent trend, with fits of $R_{\rm pred}$ versus $R_{\rm obs}$ yielding slopes of $0.3{-}0.4$, shallower than the one-to-one relation at $3.7{-}8σ$. The mean inflation is $5{-}14\%$ depending on the grid, reaching $\sim20\%$ among the largest objects. We also measure an intrinsic dispersion of $8{-}12\%$, two to three times the measurement uncertainty. While the mean inflation offset varies by a factor of three among grids, this dispersion does not, indicating that it is a property of the population and not the models. We find no correlation with mass, equilibrium temperature, orbital separation, tidal heating rate, or age, and only a marginal ($1.7σ$) trend with host-star metallicity. Most notable is the absence of any trend with irradiation, which argues against hot Jupiter-like stellar heating as the primary cause. We instead suggest that brown dwarfs span a wider range of atmospheric boundary conditions than current grids represent. The brown dwarf radius inflation is comparable to that in fully convective M dwarfs, and it is likely the transiting counterpart to the luminosity discrepancies seen in dynamical-mass benchmarks. If the trend extends below the deuterium-burning limit, it would bias directly imaged planet mass estimates, overestimating luminosity-age masses by several to ten percent and requiring spectroscopic-gravity masses to be revised upward by $\sim20{-}45\%$.
