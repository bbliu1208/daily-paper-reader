## 摘要
在早期太阳系中，非碳质（NC）和碳质（CC）星子之间存在同位素二分性。根据这些星子相对于原行星盘中冷凝线的形成位置，NC和CC分化星子可能具有不同的氧化还原状态和含水量。然而，这些差异的程度以及由此产生的NC和CC星子的吸积环境仍存在争议。在此，我们使用热演化和发电机产生模型来探索星子核大小（氧化还原状态的代理指标）和地幔含水量对星子发电机产生的影响。我们发现，与不同形成情景一致的核大小和含水量的组合，会产生磁场强度和持续时间都截然不同的星子。通过将我们的模型与现有的NC星子古地磁数据进行比较，我们认为这些天体在形成时含有少量水冰，并在分化过程中有效脱气。未来的古地磁测量可以确定CC星子是否像NC星子一样有效脱气，以及NC储库中星子形成区域的数量。总体而言，我们证明了陨石古地磁学与发电机产生模型相结合，为星子的吸积环境及其含水量的演化提供了新的见解。

## Abstract
In the early Solar System, an isotopic dichotomy existed between non-carbonaceous (NC) and carbonaceous (CC) planetesimals. Depending on the formation location of these planetesimals relative to condensation lines in the protoplanetary disk, NC and CC differentiated planetesimals could have had distinct redox states and water contents. However, the extent of these differences and the resulting accretion environments of NC and CC planetesimals are debated. Here, we use thermal evolution and dynamo generation models to explore the effect of planetesimal core size, a proxy for redox state, and mantle water content on planetesimal dynamo generation. We find that combinations of core size and water content consistent with different formation scenarios produce planetesimals with stark contrasts in both magnetic field strength and duration. By comparing our models to existing paleomagnetic data for NC planetesimals, we suggest these bodies formed with a small amount of water-ice and degassed efficiently during differentiation. Future paleomagnetic measurements could determine whether CC planetesimals degassed as efficiently as NC planetesimals and the number of planetesimal formation regions in the NC reservoir. Overall, we demonstrate that meteorite paleomagnetism combined with dynamo generation models provides novel insight into the accretion environments of planetesimals and the evolution of their water contents.
