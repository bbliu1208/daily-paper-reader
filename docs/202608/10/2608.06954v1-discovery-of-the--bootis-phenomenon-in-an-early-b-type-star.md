## 摘要
化学特殊的A型恒星中，λ Boo类恒星的特征是难熔元素的贫乏和挥发性元素的正常。它们的形成通道尚未完全确定，但它们为恒星形成过程中的恒星-盘-行星相互作用、后期系统演化中的恒星-行星相互作用以及恒星与星际介质之间的相互作用提供了见解。我们报告了在质量较大的早期B型恒星HD 37356中首次探测到λ Boo现象，HD 37356是Ori OB1c的成员。我们使用混合非局部热动平衡（non-LTE）模型大气技术分析了高质量光谱。我们利用神经网络模拟合成光谱，并结合马尔可夫链蒙特卡罗算法确定了大气参数和元素丰度。我们还使用盖亚数据和恒星演化模型推导了该恒星的基本参数。HD 37356与摩根-基南标准γ Peg非常相似，除了难熔元素的谱线系统性较弱外，光谱特征密切相关。定量分析得出相似的恒星参数。挥发性元素的丰度与宇宙标准丰度一致，而难熔元素的贫乏程度为0.25至0.45dex。特别是，较低的铁丰度解释了HD 37356与γ Peg相比不同的脉动行为。在λ Boo恒星的不同形成通道中，只有恒星形成过程中从星周盘吸积难熔元素贫乏的气体这一机制在此案例中似乎是可行的。这种情况要求HD 37356是一个真正的慢自转体，以防止丰度模式被诸如子午环流等内部混合过程抹去。

## Abstract
Chemically peculiar A-type stars of the $λ$ Boo class are characterised by depletions of refractory elements and normal volatile species. Their formation channel(s) have not yet been fully established, but they provide insight into star-disc-planet interactions during star formation, star-planet interactions in later system evolution, or interactions between stars and the interstellar medium. We report the first detection of the $λ$ Boo phenomenon in a massive early B-type star, HD 37356, a member of Ori OB1c. We analysed a high-quality spectrum using a hybrid non-LTE model-atmosphere technique. We determined atmospheric parameters and elemental abundances using neural networks to emulate synthetic spectra, coupled with a Markov chain Monte Carlo algorithm. We also derived the fundamental parameters of the star using Gaia data and stellar evolution models. HD 37356 closely resembles the Morgan-Keenan standard $γ$ Peg, closely agreeing in spectral signatures except for the lines of refractory elements, which are systematically weaker. The quantitative analysis yields similar stellar parameters. The abundances of volatile elements are compatible with cosmic standard abundances, while refractory species are depleted by 0.25 to 0.45dex. In particular, the lower iron abundance explains the different pulsational behaviour of HD 37356 compared to $γ$ Peg. Of the different formation channels for $λ$ Boo stars, only the accretion of gas depleted in refractory elements from the circumstellar disc during star formation appears to be feasible in this case. This scenario requires HD 37356 to be a true slow rotator in order to prevent the abundance pattern from being erased by internal mixing processes such as meridional circulation.
