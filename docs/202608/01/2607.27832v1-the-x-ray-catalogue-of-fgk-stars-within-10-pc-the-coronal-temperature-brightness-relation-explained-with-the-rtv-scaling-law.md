## 摘要
对恒星 X 射线发射的全面测量对于我们理解恒星发电机、系外行星大气逃逸以及原行星盘蒸发具有重要意义。我们提供了一个 10 pc 内 FGK 型主序恒星的 X 射线探测星表，该星表考虑了恒星的多重性和探测器的光学负载。为了构建该 X 射线星表，我们分析了 SRG（Spectrum Roentgen Gamma）/eROSITA 和 XMM-Newton 对 FGK 10 pc 样本中恒星的所有观测，并将样本与 ROSAT 星表进行了交叉匹配。我们对 X 射线光谱拟合了热等离子体模型，以推导 X 射线通量和日冕温度。我们研究了日冕温度与 X 射线表面通量之间的关系，发现结果与太阳日冕磁结构不同类型所覆盖的范围一致。温度-亮度关系的下限由蒙德极小期恒星 HD 166620 和另外三颗位置表明其可能是蒙德极小期候选体的恒星所定义。我们识别出由 XMM-Newton 和 eROSITA 数据推导的温度-亮度定律之间的系统性差异，并将其归因于它们不同的仪器响应函数。我们研究了短期耀发和长期活动周期对日冕温度随 X 射线亮度演化的影响。除三颗恒星外，整个样本在温度-亮度关系中观察到非常小的离散度。经验性的 kT-FX 关系的一个自然解释是由针对太阳磁回路定义的 RTV 标度律所提供的。观测到的关系的小扩展表明日冕回路的长度与填充因子之间存在普遍的标度关系。三个观测到的离群点偏离了回路长度与填充因子比值的这种不变性，其原因尚不清楚。

## Abstract
A comprehensive measurement of stellar X-ray emission has important implications for our understanding of stellar dynamos, exoplanet atmosphere loss, and evaporation of protoplanetary disks. We present a catalogue of X-ray detections of the FGK-type main-sequence stars within 10 pc, which accounts for stellar multiplicity and optical loading of the detectors. To build the X-ray catalogue, we analysed all observations from SRG (Spectrum Roentgen Gamma)/eROSITA and XMM-Newton for stars in the FGK 10pc sample, and we cross-matched the sample with ROSAT catalogues. We fit thermal plasma models to the X-ray spectra to derive X-ray fluxes and coronal temperatures. We investigate the relation between coronal temperature and X-ray surface flux and find the result to be consistent with the ranges covered by different types of solar coronal magnetic structures. The lower end of the temperature-brightness relation is defined by the Maunder minimum star HD 166620 and three other stars whose positions identify them as possible Maunder minimum candidates. We identify systematic differences between the temperature-brightness law derived from XMM-Newton and eROSITA data that we attribute to their different instrumental response functions. We study the impact of both short-term flaring and long-term activity cycles on the evolution of coronal temperature along with X-ray brightness. With the exception of three stars, a remarkably small scatter is observed in the temperature-brightness relation across the whole sample. A natural explanation of the empirical kT-FX relation is provided by the RTV scaling law defined for solar magnetic loops. The small spread of the observed relation indicates a universal scaling between the length and the filling factor of coronal loops. The three observed outliers deviate from this invariance of the ratio of loop length to filling factor for an as of yet unknown reason.
