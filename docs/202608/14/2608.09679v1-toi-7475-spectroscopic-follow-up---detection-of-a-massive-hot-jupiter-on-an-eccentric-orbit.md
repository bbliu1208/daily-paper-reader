## 摘要
我们首次对TOI-7475进行了光谱后续观测，并报告探测到一个行星质量伴星。TESS观测到周期为3.25天的凌星现象，亮度下降为0.006星等，持续时间约4.5小时。宿主恒星为F5光谱型，质量约为1.48 M$_{\odot}$。我们使用PST1望远镜收集了24个高分辨率阶梯光谱。利用Wilson-Devinney方法对TESS光度数据和我们的视向速度进行了分析。我们得到伴星的行星质量为12.0$\pm$1.0 M$_{\mathrm{jup}}$。较小的半长轴和较短的轨道周期将该行星归类为热木星。其轨道为椭圆形，偏心率为0.16。光谱数据中椭率明显，这可能表明该系统年龄较小。

## Abstract
We present the first spectroscopic follow-up for TOI-7475, and report the detection of a planetary-mass companion. Transits with a period of 3.25 days were observed by TESS. The dips in brightness are 0.006 mag and last approximately 4.5 hours. The host star is a spectral type F5 with a mass of approximately 1.48 M$_{\odot}$. We collected 24 high resolution echelle spectra using the PST1 telescope. Analysis of the TESS photometric data and our radial velocities was performed using the Wilson-Devinney method. We obtained a planetary mass of the companion of 12.0$\pm$1.0 M$_{\mathrm{jup}}$. The small semi-major axis and short orbital period classify the planet as a Hot Jupiter. Its orbit is elliptical with an eccentricity of 0.16. The ellipticity is evident in the spectroscopic data and could indicates the young age of the system.
