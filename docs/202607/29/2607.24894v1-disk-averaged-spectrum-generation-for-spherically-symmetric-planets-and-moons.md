## 摘要
本文提出一种简单高效的方法来生成盘平均行星光谱。该技术使用从行星中心偏移的不规则网格上生成的组分光谱的加权平均。这考虑了中心到临边亮度变化以及临边外的发射，这对于具有延展大气层的天体（如土星的卫星土卫六）非常重要。该方法假设发射是球对称的，并且需要足够精细的组分光谱网格以忠实捕捉径向辐射变化。以土卫六7.7μm甲烷发射为例展示了该方法。

## Abstract
Here I present a simple and efficient method for generating a disk-averaged planetary spectrum. The technique uses a weighted average of component spectra generated on an irregular grid of offsets from the planet center. This accounts for variations in center to limb brightness and emission from beyond the limb, which is important for bodies with extended atmospheres such as Saturn's moon Titan. The method assumes spherically symmetric emission and requires a grid of component spectra fine enough to faithfully capture radial radiance variations. An example application to Titan's 7.7 $μ$m methane emission demonstrates the method.

    \bar{s} = \frac{\int_0^r 2\pi x \, s(x) \, dx}{\pi r^2}

    \bar{s} \approx \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} w_i s_i
