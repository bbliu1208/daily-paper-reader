## 摘要
原行星盘的演化受到中心恒星吸积以及通过喷流和风的质量损失调节。虽然原子和电离外流常见，但演化后的Class II盘中分子风的检测仍稀少。我们表征了近乎边缘取向的Class II盘HV Tau C中分子氢（H$_2$）发射的空间、热、动力学和运动学性质，并评估了其分子风的影响。我们还利用同一中红外光谱中检测到的H I复合线约束了吸积。利用MINDS Cycle 1 GTO项目的JWST/MIRI-MRS数据，我们分析了空间分辨的纯转动H$_2$发射。转动图和位置-速度图约束了激发和运动学，并据此估计了风的性质。我们探测到延展的H$_2$发射，追踪了一个宽角双锥分子风，延伸超出近红外散射光盘、ALMA 887 $\mu$m尘埃连续谱以及致密的$^{12}$CO ($J=3$-$2$)气体盘。H$_2$转动图需要暖（~600K）和热（~2000K）成分，类似于更年轻的原恒星中的成分。气体显示出数十km s$^{-1}$的向外运动和数十到数百年的动力学时标。推断的质量损失率约为$10^{-8}$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$，而由H I线导出的吸积率为$10^{-10}$-$10^{-8}$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$。由于边缘取向几何，吸积率可能被低估。我们的结果表明，宽角分子H$_2$风可持续到Class II阶段，其外流速率的数值与一些原恒星系统相当，表明这样的风可能对去除角动量、盘演化和消散仍然重要。（摘要已修改；完整版见论文。）

## Abstract
The evolution of protoplanetary disks is regulated by accretion onto the central star and mass loss through jets and winds. While atomic and ionized outflows are commonly observed, molecular winds in evolved Class II disks remain rarely detected. We characterize the spatial, thermal, kinematic, and dynamical properties of molecular hydrogen (H$_2$) emission from the nearly edge-on Class II disk HV Tau C and assess the impact of its molecular wind. We also constrain accretion using H I recombination lines detected in the same mid-infrared spectrum. Using JWST/MIRI-MRS data from the MINDS Cycle 1 GTO program, we analyze spatially resolved pure-rotational H$_2$ emission. Rotational and position-velocity diagrams constrain excitation and kinematics, from which we estimate wind properties. We detect extended H$_2$ emission tracing a wide-angled, biconical molecular wind extending beyond the near-infrared scattered-light disk, ALMA 887 $μ$m dust continuum, and compact $^{12}$CO ($J=3$-$2$) gas disk. The H$_2$ rotational diagram requires warm ($\sim$600K) and hot ($\sim$2000K) components, similar to those in younger protostars. The gas shows outward motions of a few tens of km s$^{-1}$ and dynamical timescales of tens to hundreds of years. The inferred mass-loss rate is $\sim10^{-8}$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$, while accretion rates derived from H I lines are $10^{-10}$-$10^{-8}$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$. The accretion rate may be underestimated because of the edge-on geometry. Our results show that wide-angled molecular H$_2$ winds can persist into the Class II phase, with outflow rates comparable to some protostellar systems, suggesting that such winds may remain important for angular momentum removal, disk evolution, and dispersal. ### 8. 不足与局限
- **单一源限制**：仅研究HV Tau C一个源，无法评估分子风在Class II群体中的普遍性，结论外推有限。

- **缺少多次观测**：无法观测风的时间演化（例如与吸积爆发的关系）。
