## 摘要
大规模光谱巡天需要稳健的管线，能够从不同质量的数据中推断赫罗图（HR图）大范围内的恒星参数。SDSS-V就是这样一个巡天项目，来自低分辨率光学BOSS光谱仪的数据将提供覆盖银河系多种恒星种群的大数据集。为了更好地分析这些数据，我们提出了BOSS-CLAM，这是一个生成式前向建模管线，用于从连续归一化的BOSS光谱中推断有效温度（$T_\mathrm{eff}$）、表面重力（$\log g$）、金属丰度（$[\mathrm{Fe/H}]$）和α元素丰度（$[α/\mathrm{M}]$）。BOSS-CLAM通过多项式映射将恒星标签映射到非负矩阵分解（NMF）基向量权重上，该映射与光谱分解联合优化，为处理低分辨率BOSS数据提供了更灵活的框架。此外，训练标签来自四个互补来源（ASPCAP、BOSS-MINESweeper、宽双星和热星验证样本），使其能够覆盖从冷M矮星到热OB星以及广泛金属丰度范围。我们为1,708,214条BOSS光谱推断参数，并推荐了包含915,514个源的清洁星表。通过与疏散星团和球状星团的验证，证明了在广泛金属丰度范围内的均匀、准确丰度。宽双星测试显示，在信噪比SNR=10时，丰度不确定性为$σ_{[\mathrm{Fe/H}]} ≈ 0.15$ dex和$σ_{[α/\mathrm{M}]} ≈ 0.06$ dex。最后，我们证明BOSS-CLAM星表恢复了银河系盘已知的化学结构，非常适合银河考古学、化学标记和恒星种群建模。该管线、训练模型和星表作为SDSS-V DR20的一部分公开发布。

## Abstract
Large spectroscopic surveys require robust pipelines capable of inferring stellar parameters over a wide range of the Hertzsprung-Russell (HR) diagram from data of varying quality. SDSS-V is one such survey, where the data from the lower-resolution, optical BOSS spectrograph will provide a large dataset covering a wide range of Galactic stellar populations. To better analyze these data, we present BOSS-CLAM, a generative, forward modeling pipeline for inferring effective temperature ($T_\mathrm{eff}$), surface gravity ($\log g$), metallicity ($[\mathrm{Fe/H}]$), and $α-$abundance ($[α/\mathrm{M}]$) from continuum-normalized BOSS spectra. BOSS-CLAM maps stellar labels to Non-negative Matrix Factorization (NMF) basis vector weights via a polynomial mapping jointly optimized with the spectral decomposition, which provides a more flexible framework for working with the lower-resolution BOSS data. Additionally, training labels are drawn from four complementary sources (ASPCAP, BOSS-MINESweeper, wide binaries, and a hot star validation sample), which enables coverage from cool M dwarfs through hot OB stars, and across a wide range of metallicity. We infer parameters for 1,708,214 BOSS spectra, with a recommended clean catalog of 915,514 sources. Validation against open and globular clusters demonstrates homogeneous, accurate abundances across a wide range of metallicity. Wide binary tests yield abundance uncertainties of $σ_{[\mathrm{Fe/H}]} \approx 0.15$ dex and $σ_{[α/\mathrm{M}]} \approx 0.06$ dex at SNR = 10. Finally, we demonstrate that the BOSS-CLAM catalog recovers known chemical structure of the Milky Way disk and is well-suited for Galactic archaeology, chemical tagging, and stellar population modeling. The pipeline, trained model, and catalog are publicly released as part of SDSS-V DR20.
