## 摘要
极端碎片盘提供了巨撞击后岩石行星组装的时间域探针。我们比较了斯皮策和JWST对HD 15407A周围温暖碎片盘的观测光谱，两者相隔14.95年。在6-25μm波段，扣除恒星贡献后的中红外超量下降了14.0±1.2%，而矿物指纹几乎未变。在采用的共同简化矿物基中，富镁辉石占归一化拟合矿物权重的约65%，而二氧化硅和镁橄榄石分别贡献约22-23%和12-13%。在两个时期，约93-94%的拟合矿物权重由5.0μm的颗粒携带。拟合的光学薄表面成分振幅下降了32%，这与来自盘表面的矿物发射通量减少一致，而非新的尘埃成分出现。对IRAS至JWST的多时期比率进行拟合的简单碰撞级联模型给出的特征时间$t_c=87^{+17}_{-12}$年，经过一个$t_c$后，斯皮策归一化的尘埃超量比率达到0.5。HD 15407A与一个演化后的撞击后储库一致，该储库近期很少补充非常小的颗粒：其岩石矿物指纹至少自2008年以来保持稳定，而其温暖矿物发射通量在数百年间逐渐衰减。

## Abstract
Extreme debris disks provide time-domain probes of rocky planet assembly after giant impacts. We compare Spitzer and JWST spectra of the warm debris disk around HD 15407A, obtained 14.95 yr apart. Across 6-25$μ$m, the stellar-subtracted mid-infrared excess declined by $14.0\pm1.2$%, while the mineral fingerprint is nearly unchanged. Within the adopted common reduced mineral basis, Mg-rich pyroxene dominates the normalized fitted mineral weights at about 65%, while silica and forsterite contribute about 22-23% and 12-13%, respectively. In both epochs, about 93-94% of the fitted mineral weight is carried by 5.0$μ$m grains. The fitted optically thin surface-component amplitude drops by 32%, consistent with reduced mineral-emitting flux from the disk surface rather than the appearance of a new dust composition. A simple collisional-cascade guide fitted to the IRAS-to-JWST multi-epoch ratios gives $t_c=87^{+17}_{-12}$ yr, for which the Spitzer-normalized dust-excess ratio reaches 0.5 after one $t_c$. HD 15407A is consistent with an evolved post-impact reservoir with limited recent supply of very small grains: its rocky mineral fingerprint has remained stable since at least 2008, while its warm mineral-emitting flux is gradually fading over several hundred years.
