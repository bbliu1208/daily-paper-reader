## 摘要
展示大气金属富集的白矮星为系外行星物质的体组成提供了直接窗口，并对岩石系外行星系统的构建块提供了最直接的约束。近期研究提出磁场可能影响这些物质的吸积过程，可能引导碎片沿磁力线运动并将其保留在磁极附近，而非使其均匀重新分布于恒星表面。本文报告了磁性DZ白矮星WD1532+129的光度、光谱和分光偏振变化性，揭示出约289天的自转周期，这是迄今直接测量到的任何简并星中最长的周期。观测到的周期可能源于演化至白矮星阶段过程中的角动量损失与异常缓慢自转的前身星（可能是磁性Ap星）的联合效应。该白矮星表面存在丰度不均匀性，金属集中在两个磁极附近，观测到的调制与局限在磁极附近的富金属区域一致。WD1532+129是20秒差距内四个已知磁性DZ白矮星中的第三个显示出极区金属增强证据的案例，表明磁引导的不均匀性是磁性金属富集白矮星的常见属性。

## Abstract
White dwarfs that show atmospheric metal enrichment offer a direct window into the bulk composition of exoplanetary material and provide some of the most direct constraints on the building blocks of rocky exoplanetary systems. Recent work has suggested that magnetic fields may influence how this material is accreted, potentially channelling debris along field lines and retaining it near magnetic poles rather than allowing it to be redistributed uniformly across the stellar surface. Here we report the discovery of photometric, spectroscopic, and spectropolarimetric variability in the magnetic DZ white dwarf WD1532+129, revealing a rotation period of about 289 days, by far the longest yet directly measured for any degenerate star. The observed period may result from the combined effect of angular-momentum loss during the evolution leading to the white dwarf stage and an unusually slowly rotating progenitor, perhaps a magnetic Ap star. The white dwarf exhibits surface abundance inhomogeneities, with metals concentrated near both magnetic poles, and the observed modulation is consistent with localised metal-rich regions aligned with the magnetic geometry. WD1532+129 is the third of the four known magnetic DZ white dwarfs within 20 pc to show evidence for polar metal enhancement, indicating that magnetically guided inhomogeneities are a common property of magnetic metal-enriched white dwarfs.
