## 摘要
我们展示了Sh2-284中年轻恒星天体（YSO）的光谱巡天研究。Sh2-284是一个位于银河系反中心方向、距离较远（约4.5 kpc）、低金属丰度（Z≈1/3 Z_sun）的恒星形成区。候选YSO通过中红外Spitzer/IRAC数据结合光学和近红外测光筛选。后续光谱观测使用大双筒望远镜上的MODS完成。我们在贫金属环境中刻画了含盘天体的恒星与吸积特性，探测的恒星质量范围从约2.95 M_sun到约0.35 M_sun。这项工作首次对Sh2-284中的YSO进行了大视场（约45'×45'）光谱研究，提供了此银河系环境中恒星形成的全面图像。我们初步估算了三个低质量天体的铁丰度及有限数量天体的锂丰度，初步表征了这些恒星的化学性质。我们采用基于九个示踪物的多诊断方法，充分利用仪器的光谱覆盖范围，使用了H_alpha、H_beta、H_gamma、CaII红外三重线、Pa_eta、Pa_zeta和Pa_epsilon发射线。我们推导了关键吸积特性，并将其与邻近太阳金属丰度恒星形成区的测量值进行比较，以探索金属丰度驱动的吸积行为和星盘演化的潜在差异。我们初步发现，与太阳金属丰度样本相比，贫金属YSO的通量-通量关系呈现平坦化趋势，这一现象在所有诊断中均得到恢复。我们的观测表明，在该贫金属环境中选定的含盘YSO表现出顽强的吸积活动，吸积率范围约为2.3×10^(-10) M_sun/年至1.0×10^(-6) M_sun/年，中值约为2.2×10^(-8) M_sun/年。总体而言，这表明在亚太阳金属丰度环境中气体吸积可以高效维持。

## Abstract
We present a spectroscopic survey of young stellar objects (YSOs) in Sh2-284, a distant (~4.5 kpc), low-metallicity (Z~1/3 Zsun) star-forming region (SFR) toward the Galactic anticenter. Candidate YSOs were selected using mid-infrared Spitzer/IRAC data with optical and near-infrared photometry. Follow-up spectroscopy was conducted with MODS at the Large Binocular Telescope. We characterize the stellar and accretion properties of the disk-bearing population in a metal-poor environment, probing stellar masses from ~2.95 Msun to ~0.35 Msun. This work constitutes the first wide-field (~45'x45') spectroscopic investigation of YSOs in Sh2-284, providing a comprehensive view of star formation in this Galactic environment. We provide tentative first estimates of iron abundance for three low-mass targets and lithium abundance for a limited number of objects, offering an initial characterization of the chemical properties of these stars. We adopt a multi-diagnostic approach based on nine tracers, exploiting the spectral coverage of the instrument, using H_alpha, H_beta, H_gamma, CaII infrared triplet, Pa_eta, Pa_zeta, and Pa_epsilon emission lines. We derive key accretion properties and compare them with those measured in nearby, solar-metallicity SFRs to explore potential metallicity-driven differences in accretion behavior and disk evolution. We tentatively find indications of a flattening in the flux-flux relations of the metal-poor YSOs compared to solar-metallicity samples, a behavior that is recovered across diagnostics. Our observations indicate that the selected disk-bearing YSOs in this metal-poor environment exhibit resilient accretion activity, spanning from ~2.3x10^(-10) Msun/yr and ~1.0x10^(-6) Msun/yr, and a median rate of ~2.2x10^(-8) Msun/yr. Overall, this demonstrates that gas accretion can be efficiently sustained in sub-solar metallicity environments.
