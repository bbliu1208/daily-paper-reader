## 摘要
我们介绍了用于极大望远镜（ELT）高分辨率光谱仪ANDES的日冕仪（CORO）模块的模拟性能。ANDES旨在解决广泛科学问题，包括系外行星大气的表征。该仪器计划于2035年实现首次观测，其基线特性包括模块化光纤馈入阶梯光栅光谱仪，配备可见光与近红外超稳定光谱臂，可同时覆盖0.4-1.8微米光谱范围，光谱分辨率高达100,000。ANDES还包含一个由单级自适应光学（SCAO）模块和可插入的CORO模块供能的积分视场单元（IFU）模式，实现了高对比度成像与高色散光谱（R=100,000）的结合，用于研究系外行星特性。本文详细介绍了ANDES CORO的设计、主要特性及其在存在SCAO残余误差下的模拟性能，以通过高空间分辨率和前所未有的角分辨率的光谱成像模式探测系外行星大气。我们展示了利用ANDES性能展开（APU）模拟工具预测的对比度初步模拟结果以及预期探测性能，该工具用于评估在发射光及可能反射光中可探测的系外行星产量。还将讨论仪器的表征操作，以评估ANDES检测与系外行星大气特征相关的原子和分子信号的能力。

## Abstract
We present the simulated performance of the coronagraph (CORO) module for ANDES, the Extremely Large Telescope (ELT) high-resolution spectrograph. ANDES aims to address a broad range of science cases, including the characterization of the atmosphere of exoplanets. With a first light envisioned by 2035, the instrument baseline features a modular fiber-fed echelle spectrograph with visible and near-infrared ultra-stable spectral arms to provide a simultaneous spectral range of 0.4-1.8\,$μ$m with a spectral resolving power up to 100,000. ANDES also includes an Integral Field Unit (IFU) mode-fed by a single-conjugate adaptive optics (SCAO) module and an insertable CORO module, enabling the combination of high-contrast imaging and high-dispersion spectroscopy (R=100,000) for the study of exoplanet properties. In this contribution, the ANDES CORO design, its main features and its simulated performance are detailed in the presence of SCAO residual errors to probe exoplanet atmospheres with the spectro-imager mode at high-spatial resolution and with unprecedented angular resolution. We show the preliminary simulation results on the predicted contrast and the expected detection performance with ANDES performance unfolded (APU), the simulation tool to determine the yield of exoplanets that will be detected in emitted and possibly reflected light. The characterization operation of the instrument will be discussed to assess the ANDES ability to detect atomic and molecular signatures connected to the exoplanet atmosphere characteristics.
