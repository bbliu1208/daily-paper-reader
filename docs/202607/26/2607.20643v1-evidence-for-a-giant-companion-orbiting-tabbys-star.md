## 摘要
KIC 8462852，也称为塔比星，在其开普勒光变曲线中显示出深达20%的不规则通量变化。这些不寻常的事件没有明显的周期性。多波长观测表明这些变暗现象与凌星尘埃一致，但其起源仍不确定。主要的解释是尘埃可能来自一群系外彗星或星子碎片。TESS数据揭示了一个独特的、对称的凌星事件，与行星或褐矮星一致。这将支持外来彗星体可能源于行星轨道扰动的理论。对TESS数据以及档案和新获得的径向速度（RV）数据的分析表明，一颗伴星可能以至少约1030天的周期绕塔比星运行。结合开普勒和其他测光巡天所施加的约束，我们发现只有五个轨道周期窗口是可能的。我们进行了观测以跟进前两个窗口，显著减小了允许范围。使用系外彗星模型拟合TESS凌星效果不佳，或者需要物理上不真实的参数，这支持了巨型伴星的假说。在凌星轨道周期处检测到M=9.4^{+4.9}_{-4.1} M_Jup（3σ上限<28.0 M_Jup），显著性为2.3σ，提供了伴星的初步证据，但还需要更多的RV观测。结合半径R=1.7±0.1 R_Jup，该伴星将处于巨行星-褐矮星区域。未来的盖亚DR4天体测量将揭示该天体的性质。

## Abstract
KIC 8462852, also known as Tabby's star, shows deep, irregular flux variations up to 20 per cent in its Kepler light curve. These unusual events show no apparent periodicity. Multi-wavelength observations show the dips are consistent with transiting dust, although its origin remains uncertain. The leading explanation is that dust may originate from a family of exocomets or planetesimal fragments. TESS data reveal a unique, symmetric transit event consistent with a planet or brown dwarf. This would support the theory that incoming exocometary bodies may result from orbital perturbations by a planet. The analysis of the TESS and, archival and newly obtained radial velocity (RV) data, suggests that a companion could orbit around Tabby's star with a period of at least ~1030 d. When combined with the constraints imposed by Kepler and other photometric surveys, we find only five orbital period windows are possible. We conduct observations to follow-up the first two windows, significantly reducing the allowed ranges. Fitting the TESS transit using exocometary models produces a poor fit or the need of physically unrealistic parameters, favouring the giant companion hypothesis. A 2.3 $σ$ detection of $M=9.4^{+4.9}_{-4.1}$ M$_{\mathrm{Jup}}$ (3$σ$ upper limit < 28.0 M$_{\mathrm{Jup}}$) at the transit orbital period provides tentative evidence of the companion, but more RV observations are required. Along with the radius $R=1.7\pm0.1$ R$_{\mathrm{Jup}}$, the companion would lie in the giant planet-brown dwarf regime. In the future Gaia DR4 astrometry will reveal the nature of this object.
