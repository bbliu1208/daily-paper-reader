## 摘要
稀薄、"模糊"核在巨行星中的广泛认可以及次海王星中大量挥发性地幔的存在，使得内部建模成为一个高度简并的多维问题。为了绘制这些简并性，我们提出了fuzzycore，一个开源的静态结构积分器，具有统一的层状架构，覆盖从岩石-水-包层次海王星到富含挥发物的气态巨行星。该框架支持对固定观测值$(M_p, R_p, Z_{atm})$下的成分梯度进行密集参数扫描，从而能够跨半径谷和暖巨行星群体进行一致的正演模型探索。它在气态包层下方通过相分离的铁、岩石和水层求解流体静力平衡，使用高分辨率自适应网格模拟任意重元素梯度，并通过参数化稀释宽度来评估可变核-包层边界的结构影响。我们以演化推导的木星剖面为基准测试fuzzycore，证明当包层边界金属丰度和集成重元素预算匹配时，宏观半径对梯度的精确函数拓扑结构稳健至2.3%以内，从而允许平滑参数化密集地映射内部简并性。将其应用于次海王星区域，我们生成了一个水世界简并图谱，量化了所需的包层金属丰度和稀释宽度与假定的水质量分数之间的权衡，以重现固定的行星半径。通过将结构剖面与时间相关的能量传输和对流混合解耦，fuzzycore以互补而非替代完全演化式Henyey处理的分辨率绘制静态简并性，并作为正演模型后端，将来自JWST和Ariel的大气金属丰度先验与深层内部结构联系起来。

## Abstract
The widespread recognition of dilute, "fuzzy" cores in giant planets and massive volatile mantles in sub-Neptunes has made interior modelling a highly degenerate, multi-dimensional problem. To map these degeneracies, we present fuzzycore, an open-source static structural integrator with a unified layered architecture spanning rock-water-envelope sub-Neptunes through volatile-rich gas giants. The framework supports dense parameter sweeps over compositional gradients at fixed observed $(M_p, R_p, Z_{atm})$, enabling consistent forward-model exploration across the radius valley and the warm-giant population. It solves hydrostatic equilibrium across phase-separated iron, rock, and water layers beneath a gaseous envelope, using a highly resolved adaptive grid to model arbitrary heavy-element gradients and parameterising the dilution width to evaluate the structural impact of varying core-envelope boundaries. We benchmark fuzzycore against an evolution-derived Jupiter profile, demonstrating that when envelope boundary metallicities and integrated heavy-element budgets are matched, the macroscopic radius is robust to the exact functional topology of the gradient to within 2.3%, allowing smooth parameterisations to densely map interior degeneracies. Applying this to the sub-Neptune regime, we generate a water-world degeneracy atlas, quantifying how the required envelope metallicity and dilution width trade off against assumed water-mass fractions to reproduce a fixed planetary radius. By decoupling structural profiling from time-dependent energy transport and convective mixing, fuzzycore maps static degeneracies at resolutions that complement, rather than replace, fully evolutionary Henyey treatments, and serves as a forward-model backend for linking atmospheric metallicity priors from JWST and Ariel to deep interior architectures.
