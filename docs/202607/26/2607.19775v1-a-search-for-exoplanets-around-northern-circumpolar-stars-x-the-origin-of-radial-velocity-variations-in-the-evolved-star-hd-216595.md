## 摘要
由于恒星自身的变化（如表面对流、脉动和质量损失）会产生模拟开普勒运动的径向速度信号，因此探测演化恒星（尤其是渐近巨星分支恒星）周围的行星伴星颇具挑战性。我们基于约16年间从 Bohyunsan 光学天文台阶梯光栅光谱仪和 Las Cumbres 天文台网络机器人阶梯光栅光谱仪获取的高分辨率光谱数据，研究了AGB恒星HD 216595中观测到的长周期、低振幅径向速度变化的起源。径向速度测量揭示了一个统计上显著的567天的周期信号，该信号可由与亚恒星伴星一致的开普勒模型描述。然而，尽管在某些诊断指标中存在类似时间尺度的弱信号，但径向速度变化与恒星活动指标（包括谱线等分线、色球活动和测光变化）之间未发现强相关性。考虑到HD 216595的恒星特性以及与先前报道案例的相似性，观测到的径向速度变化可能与恒星内部过程有关，尽管不能完全排除伴星引起的起源。进一步进展将需要改进的诊断方法和更精细的建模，以区分恒星变化与真实轨道信号。

## Abstract
Detecting planetary companions around evolved stars, particularly asymptotic giant branch (AGB) stars, is challenging due to intrinsic stellar variability such as surface convection, pulsations, and mass loss, which can produce radial velocity (RV) signals that mimic Keplerian motion. We investigate the origin of long-period, low-amplitude RV variations observed in the AGB star HD 216595 based on high-resolution spectroscopic data spanning approximately 16 years obtained with the Bohyunsan Optical Astronomy Observatory Echelle Spectrograph (BOES) and the Las Cumbres Observatory Network of Robotic Echelle Spectrographs (NRES) instruments. The RV measurements reveal a statistically significant periodic signal at 567 days that can be described by a Keplerian model consistent with a substellar companion. However, no strong correlations are found between the RV variations and stellar activity indicators, including line bisectors, chromospheric activity, and photometric variability, although weak signals at similar timescales are present in some diagnostics. Given the stellar properties of HD 216595 and similarities to previously reported cases, the observed RV variations are likely related to intrinsic stellar processes, although a companion-induced origin cannot be definitively ruled out. Further progress will require improved diagnostics and more sophisticated modeling to disentangle stellar variability from genuine orbital signals.
