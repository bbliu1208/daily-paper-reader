## 摘要
行星形成始于年轻恒星周围原行星盘中的尘埃生长和星子形成。为了理解这些过程，必须从盘观测中估计尘埃颗粒大小。在本研究中，我们基于冷却时标的估计，开发了一种限制颗粒大小的新方法。该方法适用于具有倾斜内盘、在外盘上投下阴影的过渡盘，其温度变化作为尘埃特性的示踪。通过使用近红外散射光图像构建盘表面的三维模型，并将其与亚毫米尘埃连续谱图进行比较，我们估计了受辐照区域和阴影区域之间的空间偏移，以推导冷却时标。然后，我们构建了一个解析模型，在假设湍流扩散强度的情况下，计算尘埃热发射高度处的冷却时标，以推断尘埃表面密度和尘埃颗粒大小。将这种方法应用于HD 142527周围的原行星盘，我们发现盘北部阴影区域的冷却时标约为轨道周期的百分之几，且与观测一致的最大颗粒大小约为0.1-1毫米。我们还发现垂直剪切不稳定性（需要短冷却时标）所需的条件得到满足，从而产生与近红外观测一致的湍流强度。本研究证明，估计冷却时标是限制尘埃颗粒大小的有效工具。我们的方法可普遍应用于其他具有内盘诱导阴影的过渡盘。

## Abstract
Planet formation begins with dust growth and planetesimal formation within protoplanetary disks surrounding young stars. To understand these processes, it is essential to estimate dust grain sizes from disk observations. In this study, we develop a new method to constrain grain size based on the estimation of cooling timescales. Our approach applies to transitional disks that possess an inclined inner disk casting shadows on the outer disk, whose temperature variations serve as a tracer of dust properties. By constructing a three-dimensional model of the disk surface using near-infrared scattering light images and comparing it with submillimeter dust continuum maps, we estimate the spatial offset between the irradiated and shadowed regions to derive the cooling timescale. We then build an analytic model that calculates the cooling timescale at the dust thermal emission height with an assumed turbulent diffusion intensity to infer the dust surface density and dust grain size. Applying this method to the protoplanetary disk around HD~142527, we find that the disk's northern shadowed region cools on a timescale of a few percent of the orbital period and that the maximum grain size consistent with the observations is approximately 0.1-1 mm. We also find that the conditions required for the vertical shear instability, which needs a short cooling timescale, are satisfied, allowing turbulence with an intensity consistent with near-infrared observations. This study demonstrates that estimating cooling timescales is an effective tool for constraining dust grain size. Our approach can be generally applied to other transition disks with inner-disk-induced shadows.
